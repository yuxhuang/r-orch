Fixed the inactive SQLite adapter answering `snapshot_expired` to the calls queued on a snapshot whose read met a fresh fatal error: every admitted call now answers `storage_unavailable`, while idle and checkpoint expiry still answer `snapshot_expired`. Writer retry registrations are now identified by their episode rather than by the instant of its first failure, so two episodes that fail on the same clock reading no longer clear each other's retry state. The supported producer cardinality of the writer's retained retry (one) is documented and tested.
